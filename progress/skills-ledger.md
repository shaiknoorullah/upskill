# Skills ledger: the honest record

A topic only moves right when there's evidence. Statuses: assessed (from the interviews), practised (did the lab), demonstrated (explained it and showed it without help), interview-ready (did it under time pressure in /break, /interview or a checkpoint). Evidence means a date plus what happened, for example "2026-10-02: fixed Cape Town in 18 min, no hints".
