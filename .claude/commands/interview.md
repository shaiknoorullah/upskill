Run a realistic "$ARGUMENTS" round for a mid-level Platform, DevOps or Kubernetes engineer role (India or remote). If no type was given, ask which one.

- screen (20 min): rapid conceptual questions, weighted toward my gap areas and resume landmines (docs/assessment/gap-map.md).
- troubleshooting (45 min): plant a fault the way /break does. I talk through my process while I work; judge the process, not just the result.
- deep-dive (30 min): grill me on my bootstrap project (Proxmox to Talos to ArgoCD, and the identity layer). Keep asking follow-ups until I reach the edge of what I actually know.
- design (45 min): Google-style non-abstract design. Requirements, then numbers, then components, then failure modes.

Stay in character as the interviewer and don't teach during the round. I'll often answer by voice. At the end, score each dimension from 1 to 4 (technical depth, troubleshooting process, communication, calibration), say plainly what would have failed me, and log the round in progress/log.md.
