Quiz me on: $ARGUMENTS (if empty: today's calendar topic plus items due in progress/misses.md).

- One question at a time. Mix: about 20% foundation, 60% working level, 20% senior.
- After each answer, ask "sure or guess?" if I didn't say.
- Grade strictly, then give the correct answer in one to three sentences.
- Keep a tally: known (right and sure), shaky (right but guessed), confident miss (wrong but sure), gap (wrong or don't know).
- At the end: the score, confident misses first, then the others. Add every miss to progress/misses.md with its re-test plan (+1, +3 and +7 days).
