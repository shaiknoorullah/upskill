# Day 29: Rehearse out loud

- Course: Course 2, the depth month
- Why: Tomorrow is a live troubleshooting round. Today you practise saying your reasoning out loud.
- Minimum version (low-energy day): Do only the review block.

## Block 1. Subnetting speed round (Warm-up, 15 min, where: Browser)

- Do 10 problems at [subnettingpractice.com](https://subnettingpractice.com/). Target: under 20 seconds each.

Done when: 10 problems at under 20 seconds each.

## Block 2. Review every card (Review, 60 min, where: Repo and phone)

- Read all your cards in order, Days 1 to 27.
- For each topic marked ❗ on your Linux list, explain it out loud in under 60 seconds. Mark any you stumble on.
- Reread the cards for the ones you marked.

Done when: Every ❗ topic explained out loud at least once.

## Block 3. Rehearse a live round (Lab, 60 min, where: Browser)

- Redo a SadServers scenario you missed earlier. Narrate every step out loud as if an interviewer were listening.
- The rule: before each command, say what you're checking and why.

Done when: One scenario solved while narrating.

## Block 4. CKA: series Days 38–40 (CKA, 120 min, where: YouTube and clusters)

- Watch Days 38–40: troubleshooting cluster components, network troubleshooting and node maintenance, and JSONPath.
- Do the tasks for [Day 38](https://github.com/piyushsachdeva/CKA-2024/tree/main/Resources/Day38), [Day 39](https://github.com/piyushsachdeva/CKA-2024/tree/main/Resources/Day39) and [Day 40](https://github.com/piyushsachdeva/CKA-2024/tree/main/Resources/Day40).

Done when: All three tasks done.

## Block 5. Review your pipeline (Job hunt, 30 min, where: Laptop)

- Count your applications, replies and interviews in the tracker.
- If replies are low, change the roles or companies you target, and ask Claude to help you rework the resume's top section.

Done when: Pipeline numbers written down.
